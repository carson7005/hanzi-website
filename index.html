Draw each stroke in order. You get 3 hints per word; each shows only the next stroke.

Type a tone number after each syllable: ma1 mā · ma2 má · ma3 mǎ · ma4 mà · ma (or ma5) for neutral. Type v for ü. Tone marks work too.

Punctuation, capitals and small words like "a"/"the" don't matter. If your wording is different but means the same, mark it right.
